# The Mamari calendar against the named month, three counting rules

Named month: 30 nights; name-class boundaries after nights [4, 10, 18, 23, 25, 27] (the Kokore stretches [5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23] and the shared-base pairs).

The calendar proper: seven runs between the eight marker groups; 2 crescents follow the last group and are counted apart.

| rule | run lengths | nights | anchored: marker gaps on a boundary | chance | best offset | its hits | chance (best of 30 offsets) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| C: every unit but the bird 600 | 3 6 4 4 5 3 5 | 30 | 1 of 6 | 0.784 | 1 | 4 of 6 | 0.204 |

## Crescent orientation in the tracing

24 plain crescents measured by ink asymmetry (left third against right third of the glyph): 17 have more ink on the left, 7 on the right. In Barthel's reading orientation the crescents do not flip between halves of the month, so the drawing offers no waxing-to-waning anchor; the anchor used above is the first unit as night 1.
