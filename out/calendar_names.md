# The Mamari calendar against the named nights

Night names from Metraux 1940, page 50: 30 nights. Stretches sharing a base name: kokore nights 5 to 10 (6), kokore nights 19 to 23 (5), rongo nights 26 to 27 (2).

Calendar from section 21: 7 crescent runs of 2, 6, 3, 2, 5, 3, 5, sum 26, plus 2 after the last marker group; marker groups after crescents 2, 8, 11, 13, 18, 21.

## The two longest runs

The calendar's two longest runs are 6 and 5 crescents, in that order. The month's two Kokore stretches are 6 and 5 nights, in that order. Among 20000 random divisions of 26 crescents into 7 runs, a run of exactly 6 followed later by a run of exactly 5 occurred in 8.0%.

## Marker groups at name-class boundaries

Boundaries of the month, gaps where a shared-name stretch begins or ends: after nights 4, 10, 18, 23, 25, 27. With crescents taken as nights in order and every offset tried, the best offset is 2, placing 3 of 6 interior marker groups on a boundary. Random marker placement, best offset likewise chosen, reaches at least 3 in 40.5% of trials.
