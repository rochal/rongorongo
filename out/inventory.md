## Reference points

- Rapa Nui has about 55 syllables (10 consonants x 5 vowels, plus bare vowels). A pure syllabary would need roughly that many signs, and almost every sign would recur.
- Linear B, a syllabary with some logograms, has about 90 signs; Cypriot about 55.
- Logographic and mixed scripts run to hundreds or thousands of signs with a long tail of rare ones: Maya about 800 known signs, Egyptian about 700 in the classical period, Chinese several thousand.
