# Compound decomposition

Basic set: the 55 most frequent head signs with drawings, 63% of head-sign tokens (one witness per family). Targets: the other 547 drawn signs. Tolerance 1 px; a placement needs F1 >= 0.3 and must explain >= 10% of the target's ink; a target decomposes at coverage >= 80% and precision >= 70% with up to 3 parts.

## Positive control

33 variant drawings that Barthel gave for basic signs, fitted with the basic templates: mean coverage 0.96, median 0.97; 42% matched their own sign first; 64% reach the decomposition threshold.

If every decomposing sign were replaced by its parts, the head inventory would fall from 602 to 445 drawn signs, and 1070 of 3168 rare tokens (34%) would be rewritten with basic signs.
