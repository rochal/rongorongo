# What the hand pass over the boxes has turned up

Observations made while correcting glyph boxes on the prints and tracings with `scripts/box_editor.py`. Each is a place where the carved glyphs, Barthel's tracing and the CEIPP transliteration do not say the same thing. They are candidates for the allograph list and for the sign dossier, not results; none has been tested beyond the eye and, where noted, the shape descriptor.

| Side, line, positions | Transliteration | On the wood | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ev04, 21 to 23 | 607, 607, 650y | three bird figures, the third with a small hook on its right | 650 occurs three times in the corpus, always as 650y. The hook is what Barthel's tracing adds; the descriptor sees it (0.21 against the 607s, where 607s score 0.39 among themselves). Candidate: 650 as 607 with an attachment. |
| Ev04, 25 to 26 | 200.070.003, 380.001 | the 003 component of the compound is carved a little apart from its head | The count alignment and a first hand pass both gave the detached stroke a box of its own. It is part of unit 25. |
| Ev05, 29 to 31 | 380.001, 680, 684 | a small hooked stroke, then two identical two-headed figures | Barthel's 680 series splits the two-headed figure into several numbers by small details; here the two carved figures are the same. Candidate: 680 and 684 as one sign. The "380.001" is a hooked stroke on the tracing, a plain stroke on the print, read as a seated figure with a stroke. |
| Ev08, 31 to 32 | 068.010, 077.254 | on the tracing, one connected figure: a hooked element and a raised-arms figure joined by a wavy base line; on the print the figure stands clear of its neighbours and the base is not visible at this resolution | Barthel joins what the photograph does not show joined; 254 is presumably the base. Around these positions the tracing's count alignment is a step off (its box 29 holds the three circles the transliteration calls 30), so the line needs the hand pass on the tracing. |
| Ev07, after 6 | 035, then 071.065.071 | a dotted branch of the 070 kind, six round tips, standing between the two | Nine carved figures where the transliteration has eight units, on the print and on the tracing alike. Barthel's 065 is a forked leaf, not dots, so the branch is not a component of the compound. The transliteration has no unit for it; the box count follows the transliteration, so the branch is folded into the box of the compound at 7 and recorded here. |
